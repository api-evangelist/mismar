# MisMar

> MisMar (مسمار) is a Saudi Arabian automotive maintenance and repair platform that connects vehicle owners with certified service centers. It operates a consumer mobile app (B2C) alongside a Network-as-a-Service (NaaS) platform and Workshop Management System (WMS) serving fleets (B2B) and government (B2G). MisMar is a portfolio company of 500 Global. No public developer API, OpenAPI, or SDK surface is currently published.

## About
- Industry: Automotive aftermarket / mobile car maintenance and repair
- Segments: B2C (car owners), B2B (fleets), B2G (government), Insurance
- Coverage: 45+ Saudi cities; at-home or center-based service
- Products: fault diagnostics, accident restoration, oil/battery/tire services, periodic maintenance, car wash, protective treatments; Gold warranty up to 5 years
- Backed by: 500 Global
